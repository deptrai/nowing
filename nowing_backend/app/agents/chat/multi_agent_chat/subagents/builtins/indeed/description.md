Indeed job market specialist: pulls job postings from Indeed (US market). Returns job title, company, location, salary text, remote/hybrid/on-site status, posting date, and apply URL. Use whenever the task is to research hiring trends, competitor headcount, salary signals, or job market data on Indeed. Triggers include "software engineer jobs in remote US", "Indeed data analyst salaries", "companies hiring product managers on Indeed", and similar. Not a job application tool — do not apply, submit CVs, or contact recruiters on the user's behalf.
