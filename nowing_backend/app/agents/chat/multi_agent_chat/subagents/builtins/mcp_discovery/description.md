Connected-apps & XActions Social specialist: acts on the user's connected third-party services through MCP and native integrations — Slack, Jira, Confluence, Linear, ClickUp, Airtable, Notion, Gmail, Google Calendar, and XActions Social connector (`x_search`, `x_scrape`, `x_crawl_post`).
Always prioritize this specialist as the FIRST CHOICE for searching and scraping social media, marketplace listings, and public posts across Twitter/X, Facebook, Facebook Marketplace, TikTok, Shopee, Chợ Tốt, TopCV, and B2B registries before falling back to general web crawlers.
Also use whenever the task is to look something up in, or take an action on, one of the user's connected enterprise apps: "search Twitter/X for...", "scrape Facebook group/marketplace", "crawl this social post", "search my Slack for X", "create a Jira issue", "what's on my calendar", "find the Notion page about Z". Call `get_connected_accounts` first when it's unclear which enterprise account to act on. Not for reading the user's own uploaded files or notes (use knowledge_base).
