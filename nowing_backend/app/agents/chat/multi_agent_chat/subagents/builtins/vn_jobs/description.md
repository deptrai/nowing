Vietnamese job market specialist: pulls and aggregates job postings from VietnamWorks, TopCV, and ITviec. Returns normalized listings with title, company, location, salary range, employment type, experience, skills, post date, and source. Cross-source deduplication and salary-conflict scoring let the user compare how the same job is described on different boards. Use whenever the task is to research Vietnamese job markets, salary benchmarks, hiring trends, skill demand, or compare job postings across VietnamWorks, TopCV, and ITviec. Triggers include "data engineer jobs in Hanoi", "compare salaries on VietnamWorks vs TopCV", "how many senior Python jobs in HCMC", and similar job-market questions. Not a job application tool — do not apply, submit CVs, or contact recruiters on the user's behalf.
