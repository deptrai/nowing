# Presentation Studio

Nowing's **Presentation Studio** turns a chat prompt into slide decks and structured meeting minutes, so you can move from research to shareable output without leaving the conversation.

## Slide deck generation

Describe the presentation you need, and Nowing generates a deck using the Marp / PPTX pipeline:

1. Open a chat in your workspace.
2. Ask for a presentation, for example:

   > Create a 10-slide pitch deck for our B2B lead intelligence product, covering problem, solution, market size, traction, and pricing.

3. Nowing produces a structured slide deck.
4. Export the result as **PPTX** or view it as formatted slides.

## Meeting minutes with speaker diarization

For recorded meetings, Nowing can:

- Transcribe audio with speaker diarization (who spoke when).
- Summarize key decisions, action items, and open questions.
- Output a structured meeting minutes document.

This is useful for stand-ups, sales calls, workshops, and interviews.

## How to use it

1. Start a new chat in your workspace.
2. Ask for a presentation or meeting minutes in plain language.
3. Provide any source material — documents, web search results, or pasted notes.
4. Ask for revisions until the output matches your tone and structure.
5. Export or copy the final artifact.

## Output formats

| Format | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| PPTX | Sharing with stakeholders, presenting offline |
| Marp Markdown | Version control, collaboration, quick edits |
| Meeting minutes document | Follow-up emails, CRM notes, wiki pages |

## Tips

- Be specific about slide count, audience, and tone.
- Reference pinned documents or project context to ground the content.
- For meeting minutes, upload or link the audio recording when supported.
