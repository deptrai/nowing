# Lead Intelligence & Outbound

Nowing includes a **Lead Intelligence** module for finding, scoring, verifying, and reaching out to B2B leads — with a focus on Vietnamese and SEA markets.

## What it does

The lead pipeline covers the full outbound workflow:

1. **Lead discovery** — find companies and decision makers from websites, job boards, social signals, and public registries.
2. **Lead scoring** — rank leads by fit, intent signals, and contactability.
3. **Contact verification** — waterfall phone and email verification, including Vietnamese phone normalization and DNC (Do Not Call) compliance checks.
4. **Outreach automation** — multi-channel drip campaigns across email, Telegram, Zalo, and more.
5. **CRM sync** — one-click sync to Lark Base, Google Sheets, and other CRMs.

## Data sources

Nowing can enrich leads from multiple sources:

- Public business registries (e.g., masothue.com)
- Job boards (VietnamWorks, TopCV, ITviec, LinkedIn public guest data)
- E-commerce and social platforms (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop)
- Real estate listings (chotot.vn, muaban.net, batdongsan.com.vn)
- Company websites and LinkedIn public profiles

## Lead Clipper

The **Lead Clipper** lets you capture a lead from any web page in one click. It extracts:

- Company name and website
- Decision makers and titles
- Contact points
- Social profiles

Captured leads flow directly into your workspace lead pipeline.

## Multi-channel drip campaigns

Create sequences that:

- Wait a configured delay between touches.
- Branch on reply or open signals.
- Send through the best available channel for each contact.
- Track credit spend per step.

Campaigns are managed from the **Automations** section in your workspace.

## DNC compliance

Nowing includes a **Do Not Call (DNC)** compliance engine:

- Smart whitelist tracking
- Per-contact DNC status
- Compliance logging for audit

## CRM integrations

Leads can be pushed to:

- Lark Base
- Google Sheets
- Generic webhook / REST destinations
- The Nowing **Team CRM Pipeline** for shared workspace management

## How to get started

1. Open **Leads** from your workspace sidebar.
2. Create a new lead manually, import a list, or use the Lead Clipper.
3. Enrich the lead with one of the built-in enrichment tools.
4. Verify contact details.
5. Add the lead to a **drip campaign** or **CRM sync** automation.
